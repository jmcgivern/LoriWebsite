How Reflexology Therapy Works

Explore the principles behind reflexology therapy, from its historical roots to how reflex points may support whole-body wellness.

A Brief Overview

History

Reflexology has roots in ancient healing traditions and developed into a modern therapeutic approach based on the idea that specific points on the feet, hands, and face correspond to different areas of the body.

Reflex Maps

Practitioners use mapped zones and reflex points on the feet and hands to focus pressure on areas associated with organs, glands, and systems throughout the body.

Nervous System Connection

While reflexology is not a substitute for medical treatment, many people find that mindful, targeted pressure can encourage relaxation, support nervous system regulation, and promote a sense of balance.

Personalized Care

Each session is tailored to the individual. Lori considers your health history, discomforts, and goals to provide a treatment that feels supportive and comfortable.

Gentle Pressure

Techniques are adjusted based on comfort level, symptom focus, and the body’s response during the session.

Whole-Body Wellness

The goal is to support relaxation, circulation, and a grounded sense of overall well-being.

Continuing Education

Reflexology is a practice rooted in ongoing learning, observation, and individualized care.
