Reflexology Services

Hand Reflexology

Pressure is applied to various reflex points in the hands to alleviate symptoms in other areas of the body.

Foot Reflexology

Using specific hand and finger techniques, the foot is stimulated to relieve health conditions in other parts of the body.

Face Reflexology

By applying pressure to specific points of the face, corresponding organs and systems in the body are targeted. Lori begins facial sessions with manual lymph drainage, then reflexology on the decolletage, neck, and face, followed by gua sha and facial cupping.

About Reflexology

Hand, foot, and face reflexology are holistic therapies based on the idea that specific points on the hands, feet, and face correspond to organs and systems throughout the body. Reflexology therapists are specialists who undergo specific training and certification, different from that of massage therapists.
