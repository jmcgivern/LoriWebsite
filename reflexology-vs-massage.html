Reflexology Therapy
vs Massage

Discover the key differences between reflexology and massage therapy, and how they can both support your wellness journey.

Key Differences

Reflexology Therapy

  • Focus: Reflex zones on feet, hands, and face
  • Pressure: Targeted, specific pressure point work
  • Movement: Localized stimulation of reflex areas
  • Purpose: Support overall body systems and natural healing
  • Session Focus: Whole-body wellness through targeted zones

Massage Therapy

  • Focus: Muscles and soft tissues throughout the body
  • Pressure: Variable pressure, kneading, and stretching
  • Movement: Broad strokes and muscle manipulation
  • Purpose: Release muscle tension and improve circulation
  • Session Focus: Localized muscle relief and relaxation

Which Is Right for You?

Choose Reflexology if you:

  • Want to support overall wellness and natural balance
  • Prefer targeted pressure point work
  • Are looking for stress relief and nervous system support
  • Want a gentle approach that can be done fully clothed

Choose Massage if you:

  • Have specific muscle tension or sports injuries
  • Prefer hands-on manipulation of larger muscle groups
  • Want localized pain relief and muscle recovery
  • Enjoy deeper, broader tissue work

Many clients benefit from both therapies! Reflexology and massage complement each other beautifully in a comprehensive wellness routine.
