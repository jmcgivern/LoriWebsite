About reflexology therapy

reflexology therapy is a gentle, targeted therapy that supports natural balance and relaxation through stimulation of reflex zones on the feet, hands, and face.

How reflexology therapy Works

reflexology therapy is based on the idea that zones on the feet, hands, and face correspond to organs and systems throughout the body. By applying pressure to these reflex points, reflexology therapy supports the nervous, circulatory, and lymphatic systems.

Foot reflexology therapy

Foot reflexology therapy is the most common method and allows deep stimulation of key reflex zones while clients relax fully.

Hand reflexology therapy

Hand reflexology therapy is a practical option when footwork is not suitable, offering precise care in a smaller area.

Face reflexology therapy

Facial reflexology therapy combines lymphatic support, gua sha, and reflex point work to help with tension, sinus relief, and skin wellness.

Benefits Clients Notice

  • Decreased stress and anxiety
  • Improved circulation and energy flow
  • Enhanced relaxation and sleep quality
  • Support for digestive and hormonal balance

Who Can Benefit

reflexology therapy is gentle and adaptable to a wide range of clients, including people with workplace stress, chronic tightness, sleep challenges, and those seeking proactive wellness care.
