Your First Visit

New Patient Experience

From your first booking to your follow-up, Lori makes every step comfortable, clear, and confidential.

Easy Booking and Personalized Intake

Before Your Visit

Book online or by phone, then complete a short health intake form. Please wear comfortable clothing and arrive a few minutes early so your session can begin smoothly.

Your Initial Consultation

Lori reviews your health history, asks about your wellness goals, and explains reflexology techniques that best support your needs.

During the Session

Sessions are gentle and respectful. Lori checks in throughout, adjusts pressure, and focuses on comfort so you can relax completely.

Aftercare Support

After your session, you receive practical suggestions for self-care, hydration, and follow-up visits to help sustain your results.

What to Bring

Comfortable clothing, a list of medications, and any questions you have about your health history.

Session Length

Most appointments last 60 to 75 minutes, including consultation time and reflexology treatment.

Privacy & Safety

The clinic is a calm, private space where your comfort, confidentiality, and consent are always prioritized.
